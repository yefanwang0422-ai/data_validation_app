Business Data Flow Map

This diagram explains the purpose of each stage in the end-to-end process, from collecting data to producing business insights. It avoids technical details and focuses on what each step does for the business.
1 Input

Collect Data

Bring in data from business systems, uploaded files, and partners.

2 Traceability

Store Original Data (Bronze)

Keep a copy of data exactly as received so it remains fully traceable and auditable.

3 Standardization

Standardize & Organize (Silver)

Align formats and field names so datasets work together consistently.

4 Business Rules

Apply Business Scope

Decide what counts for analysis (in-scope vs out-of-scope) and record the reason.

5 Understanding

Understand Data Health

Summarize completeness and spot anomalies and duplicates so issues are visible early.

6 Quality

Quality Checks

Classify each record as valid, needs review, or invalid using business-friendly checks.

7 Completion

Fill Gaps with AI (when needed)

Enrich or complete key fields and route uncertain cases to a review queue.

8 Output

Publish Analysis-Ready Data (Gold)

Produce a trusted, auditable dataset ready for reporting, optimization, and analytics.

Business rule reminder: “Out of scope” records are not deleted. They remain available for audit, but are excluded from business KPIs and insights unless explicitly included.

Business Insights & KPIs

Dashboards and metrics built from in-scope, analysis-ready data.

Exceptions & Review Queue

A prioritized list of issues and records needing confirmation or correction.

Exports

Downloadable outputs for downstream tools, partners, or scenario modeling.

Stage Definitions (Plain English)

Collect Data

Bring in data from your sources.

Store Original Data (Bronze)

Keep what arrived unchanged so it can always be referenced.

Standardize & Organize (Silver)

Make datasets consistent so they can be analyzed together.

Apply Business Scope

Mark which records should count for business analysis and why.

Understand Data Health

Summarize patterns and flags to quickly see data quality risks.

Quality Checks

Separate reliable records from those needing review.

Fill Gaps with AI

Enrich missing information and route uncertain items for review.

Publish Analysis-Ready Data (Gold)

Deliver trusted data that powers insights, exports, and decisions.
